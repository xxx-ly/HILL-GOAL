FAQ

What is Blockchain?

Blockchain is a Decentralized Distributed Ledger, or in more simple words, a type of decentralized database. Every transaction will be recorded in the ​blocks​ and linked together with the chain​ in which it uses the hash algorithm and then ​distributes​ it to every node in the network. Each data or transaction is digitally signed to ensure its authenticity and makes it immutable, so the node itself and the existing transactions within the blocks are assumed to be of high integrity.

Which programming language is used in blockchain?

Blockchain development commonly involves programming languages like:
  • Solidity (for Ethereum smart contracts).
  • JavaScript (for front-end and back-end integration).
  • Python (for blockchain-related applications and smart contracts).
  • Go (Golang) (used in Hyperledger and Ethereum).
  • Rust (for Solana and Polkadot blockchain development).
  • C++ (used in Bitcoin and other blockchain protocols).
Each language serves specific blockchain needs depending on the project or platform.

What are the 4 types of blockchain?

The four types of blockchain are:
  • Public Blockchain: Open to anyone and fully decentralized (e.g., Bitcoin, Ethereum).
  • Private Blockchain: Restricted access, controlled by a single entity.
  • Consortium Blockchain: Managed by a group of organizations.
  • Hybrid Blockchain: Combines elements of both public and private blockchains, offering flexibility in control and access.

What are the benefits of blockchain?

Blockchain offers several transformative advantages:
  • Enhanced Security: Its decentralized nature and cryptographic algorithms make data manipulation nearly impossible, ensuring high security.
  • Immutability: Once recorded, data cannot be altered, fostering trust among users.
  • Cost Reduction: Eliminates middlemen, reducing transaction and administrative fees.
  • Decentralized Control: Distributed networks prevent centralized power, minimizing risks of fraud or manipulation.
These features position blockchain as a game-changer in various sectors, particularly those requiring secure, transparent, and efficient processes.
